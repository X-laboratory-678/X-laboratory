## For Prospective Students

If your interests align with the laboratory's work, begin by reviewing our research areas, recent publications, and current projects. A research-related inquiry may include a brief introduction, academic background, research interests, relevant experience, and a CV where appropriate. These suggestions are not formal admission requirements.

## Research Collaboration

We welcome academic discussions and research collaboration related to our research areas. Please contact the principal investigator with a concise description of the topic you would like to discuss.
